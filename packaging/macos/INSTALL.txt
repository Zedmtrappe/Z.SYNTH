ZYNTH for macOS - standalone app, VST3 and Audio Unit
(Apple Silicon and Intel, macOS 11 or later)
================================================================================

This is a test build. It is not signed with an Apple Developer ID or
notarised, so macOS blocks it until you allow it once.

PLUG-INS (VST3 + AU)
  1. Right-click "Install Plug-Ins.command" > Open > Open.
     It copies ZYNTH.vst3 and ZYNTH.component into
         ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3
         ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components
     and clears the download quarantine.
  2. Restart your DAW and rescan plug-ins.
       Logic / GarageBand: Instrument slot > AU Instruments > ZYNTH > ZYNTH
       Ableton Live:       Plug-Ins > VST3 (or AU) > ZYNTH
       (Ableton: Settings > Plug-Ins > enable "Use VST3 / Audio Units", Rescan)

  Manual alternative: copy the two files from the Plug-Ins folder to the
  folders above, then in Terminal run:
      xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/ZYNTH.vst3
      xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/ZYNTH.component

STANDALONE APP
  1. Drag ZYNTH.app into Applications (optional).
  2. Allow it once: right-click ZYNTH.app > Open > Open,
     OR in Terminal:  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/ZYNTH.app
  3. Options (top-left) > Audio/MIDI Settings: choose your output and MIDI input.

Your own presets are shared by the app and the plug-ins and are stored in:
    ~/Library/Application Support/ZYNTH/Presets
(Presets saved by the earlier "Z.SYNTH" builds are copied there automatically.)
